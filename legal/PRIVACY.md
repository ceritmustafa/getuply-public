# GetUply Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 4, 2026**

GetUply ("the app") is an alarm clock app for iOS developed by Mustafa Cerit
("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short
version: **GetUply has no accounts, no ads, and no cross-app tracking. Your
alarms, mission data, photos, and statistics stay on your device. Apple and
RevenueCat process subscription purchase history only to provide and analyze
GetUply Pro access.**

## Data we do NOT collect

- We do not require or offer any account or sign-up.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
- We do not use advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
- We do not sell or rent personal data.
- We have no first-party server that receives your alarm, mission, photo, or
  wake-statistics data. RevenueCat processes the limited subscription data
  described below as our service provider.

## Data stored only on your device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is never
uploaded anywhere by us:

- Your alarms, wake missions, settings, and wake statistics (streaks,
  completion times).
- Reference photos you capture for photo missions and custom wake-screen
  photos you pick from your library.
- Paired NFC tag and QR code identifiers used to dismiss alarms.

Deleting the app deletes this data. iCloud device backups may include it under
your own Apple account, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Permissions the app may request

Each permission is used only for the feature named, processed on device, and
optional unless you use that feature:

- **Notifications** — ringing your alarms and wake-mission reminders.
- **Camera** — scanning QR codes / barcodes, verifying photo missions, and
  counting push-up or squat repetitions. Images are analyzed on device and
  are not uploaded.
- **Motion & Fitness** — counting steps or shakes for movement missions.
- **Speech Recognition / Microphone** — voice missions; recognition runs on
  device where iOS supports it.
- **NFC** — reading the tag you paired to dismiss an alarm.
- **Photo Library** — choosing a custom wake-screen photo.
- **Screen Time (Family Controls)** — the optional App Block feature asks iOS
  to restrict distracting apps during a mission. This is enforced by iOS; we
  never see which apps you use.

## Purchases and RevenueCat

GetUply Pro payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never
receive or store your payment-card details. The app uses RevenueCat as a service
provider to validate App Store receipts, manage the anonymous Pro entitlement,
and provide subscription analytics.

RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription history, an anonymous app-user
identifier, and limited device/app technical information required to operate
that service. We do not assign a name, email address, or other account identity
to that identifier. This information is used for app functionality and
subscription analytics, not advertising or cross-app tracking. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) for details.
You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

## Content downloads

Optional alarm sounds and wake-screen artwork are downloaded from our public
asset repository hosted on GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub's servers see
standard connection metadata (such as your IP address) and handle it under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
The requests contain no identifiers from the app, and we cannot see them.

## Children

GetUply is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
any data from anyone — including children.
